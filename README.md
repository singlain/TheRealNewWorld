# <font color="#FCD748">Features</font>
- **Create & Create Aeronautics 🚂**
- **Immersive Engineering 🚀**
- **New Combat System 🗡️**
- **Progression Rebalances ⚖️**
- **Transportation Rebalances 🐎**
- **Sound & Atmosphere Improvements ✨**
- **...and more! 📌**

Being a spiritual successor of my previous, long forgotten modpack I made for a server of friends - **The Real New World** was remade from ground up for **NeoForge** and had preserved the charm from its predecessor, bringing along with lots of great additions and improvements.

Hundreds of hours went into carefully picking each mod and fine-tuning each config to result in the most balanced and fun experience!

## <font color="#FCD748">Controls ⌨️</font>

- Press <font color="#FCD748">**LSHIFT+R**</font> to toogle the recipe viewer's visibility
- Press <font color="#FCD748">**X**</font> to feint when you're about to swing a weapon 
- Press <font color="#FCD748">**C**</font> to attach a cart onto a mount or yourself
- Press <font color="#FCD748">**V**</font> to toogle slow riding while the cart is attached

<details>
<summary>Details</summary>

<summary>Changes</summary>

Ever since 1.14 trading in this game has been broken to the point of you being able to obtain the best enchantment in the game within 5 minutes of creating a new world. Mojang tried addressing it with their experiment called "Villager Trade Rebalance" that among other things, locks the most precious enchantments Librarian villagers are selling to be biome-exclusive though they weren't brave enough to make it a part of the game, making it an optional datapack instead. Despite those being the main changes, there are many more subtler ones. Read about them [here.](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Villager_Trade_Rebalance)

#### <font color="#FCD748">Raid Farms Nerf ⚖️</font>

One more change that broke the balance was 1.14's unintentional addition of Raid farms, meaning you can now obtain infinite amounts of Totems of Undying. What was meant to be a special item allowing you to cheat death now is a commodity.

Well, not anymore! While Raid Farms can still grant you saddles, axes and emeralds, they shall no longer drop Totems of Undying, making it special again.

#### <font color="#FCD748">Structure Spawnrates ⚖️</font>

- **Villages** are now 2 times more rare!
- **Pillager Outposts** are now 3 times more rare!
- **Shipwrecks** are now 3 times more rare!
- **Monuments** are now 4 times more rare!

#### <font color="#FCD748">Eye of Ender Recipe ⚖️</font>

Now in order to get to The End you'd not only need to kill a bunch of blazes and endermen, but also visit an Ancient City, a Monument and a Trial Chamber to obtain each of their signature items to craft an Eye of Ender. Good luck!

#### <font color="#FCD748">Easy Anvils & Easy Magic ⚖️</font>

Enchantment and Repair systems of this game were broken ever since they first introduced. While impossible to fully fix without a full overhaul, those are the changes that can make it less annoying while also favoring risk-free repairing your enchanted tools instead of rushing for Mending.

- You now can reroll enchantments at a cost of 5 exp and 1 lapis lazuli
- "Too Expensive!" error when trying to repair / enchant / rename an item is now gone
- Made repair costs per item capped at 8 levels

See more about these two mods [here](https://modrinth.com/mod/easy-magic/) and [there](https://modrinth.com/mod/easy-anvils/)

<details>
<summary>Performance ❗</summary>

Considering running both Create and Create Aeronautics equals running two separate rendering engines at the same time plus Distant Horizons would equal a rather abysmal performance regardless of the amount of perfrormance mods shipped alongside it.

So, how can you get around it?

1. **Pausing performance-heavy mods;** luckily, I've already did that for you - by default Distant Horizons will have its distant generation setting set to false and the CPU impact set to low. A big guide on how to tackle Distant Horizons in its current state will be down in the according section of this page.
2. **More RAM;** the bare minumum is at least 4 GB, but it's always best to allocate 8 GB instead. Minecraft is a very weird game RAM-wise and allocating anywhere past 8 either gives no performance benefit or downright worsens the performance.
3. **When playing Singleplayer, running a dedicated server and playing on localhost instead of an integrated server;** it might sound weird, but heavy modpacks like these tend to perform better if you make a server-pack and run in when you want to play the game, joining it via localhost.
4. **Pregenerating chunks;** Distant Horizons has a built-in chunk pregenerator that is described in the guide below. The main TPS eater in Minecraft after complex contraptions and farms is terrain generation. The more you pregenerate, the less stutters and lag you will have in your way. Yeah, a band-aid, I know, but that's just how modded Minecraft is, isn't it?

</details>


<details>
<summary>Distant Horizons 🏞️</summary>

As mentioned before, by default, Distant Horizons' world generator is disabled to reduce the CPU strain and load the LODs and the world gradually. Enabling therefore mentioned world generator will result in poor client performance, poor server performance or both. Below you can see the instuctions for generating LODs and chunks around you in the most efficient way possible.

##### _Please keep in mind that Distant Horizons is still in Beta and **WILL** have __some__ performance issues regardless of how well you tweak it._

1. Find a safe place to AFK in; ideailly in the light-dominant area indoors so the Phantoms and Monsters can't disturb you *
2. Press ESC for the Pause Menu
3. Open the mod menu by pressing **Mods** button
4. Go to the top-left corner and search for **Distant Horizons**
5. Press the **Config** button
6. Crank the CPU Load option to be **5. I Paid For The Whole CPU**
7. Change **Enable Distant Generation** setting to be **True**
8. (Optional) Change **Show Generation Progress** option from **Disabled** to **Overlay** to see the distant generation progress logged on your screen to know when it's finished
9. AFK until the Overlay Text from Distant Horizons' Distant Generator vanishes
10. Follow steps from 2 to 5 once more
11. Change CPU Load option from **5. I Paid For The Whole CPU** to anything from **1. Minimal Impact** to **3. Balanced** depending on how beefy your PC is. **
12. Change "Enable Distant Generation" setting to be **False**
13. (Optional) if you followed step 8, set **Show Generation Progress** setting to "False"
14. Done!

###### * If you're in Singleplayer, you can just wait through the process in the Pause Menu
###### ** Keep in mind, lower CPU Load options will drastically lower the speed of LODs generating and showing up when loading into the world.

<details>
<summary>Servers</summary>

If you're a server owner or have OP rights, you can run commands to use the distant generator regardless of your settings. Keep in mind that your server might experience severe lagging when doing so.

```
/dh pregen start <dimension> <x> <z> <radius in chunks>
```

###### You can find more info about this on the Distant Horizons user guide [here.](https://gitlab.com/distant-horizons-team/distant-horizons/-/wikis/1-user-guide/1-frequently-asked-questions/5-server-owners/Server-Owners)
